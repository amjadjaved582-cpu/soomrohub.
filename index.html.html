Fragrance families

Fresh — Citrus, aquatic; Floral — rose, jasmine, peony; Oriental — amber, vanilla, spice; Woody — cedar, sandalwood, oud. Each family carries moods and occasions — day vs night, casual vs formal. Our collection includes samples from each family.

How to apply & make it last

  1. Apply to pulse points: wrists, inner elbows, behind ears.
  2. Moisturize skin beforehand to improve longevity.
  3. Store bottles in a cool, dark place away from sunlight.
